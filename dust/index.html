What we do

High-performing leadership teams aren't hired. They're built.

We help you build them: coaching for the founders and executives already carrying the company, from people who have actually run one. Doers, not talkers. That's the filter every person in the collective clears before they get in.

Coaching for founders and their teams

For when the job outgrows the person doing it

As a company grows, the instincts that built it can start working against its leaders. Decisions get harder, teams get bigger, and there are fewer people inside the company to talk it through with. Coaching gives founders, and the teams around them, somewhere to work through that with someone who has run a company before. Honest, sometimes uncomfortable, always worth it.

  • Coaches who've built and run companies, not just studied them
  • Works for one leader, or the whole team
  • Confidential, and reachable between sessions
