A collective of C-suite operators and trained coaches

The job of leadership is changing faster than the way we hire leaders.

Nvidia is growing at a pace few companies of its size have ever sustained. New teams form every quarter, and many people now lead organisations far larger than anything they have run before, often within months of stepping up. The instincts that made someone an outstanding engineer or manager are rarely the ones that carry a much bigger org. Squadra’s coaches work with leaders at exactly this point, helping them build the judgment, confidence and shared language to lead what they’ve just been handed.

What we do

High-performing leadership teams aren't hired. They're built.

We help you build them: coaching for the leaders already carrying the organisation, from people who have actually run one. Doers, not talkers. That's the filter every person in the collective clears before they get in.

Coaching for leaders and their teams

For when the job outgrows the person doing it

As a company grows, the instincts that built it can start working against its leaders. Decisions get harder, teams get bigger, and there are fewer people inside the company to talk it through with. Coaching gives leaders, and the teams around them, somewhere to work through that with someone who has run a company before. Honest, sometimes uncomfortable, always worth it.

  • Coaches who've built and run companies, not just studied them
  • Works for one leader, or the whole team
  • Confidential, and reachable between sessions
