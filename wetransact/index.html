01 · Mandate context

Where WeTransact is, and what this coaching is for.

WeTransact is the go-to-market platform that helps software companies list on, co-sell through and resell via Microsoft Marketplace. Profitable and built without venture capital, it serves more than 500 customers with a team spread across 12 countries. Its three founders previously ran more than 200 marketplace projects for Microsoft.

Four members of the C-suite are offered one-to-one coaching: Johan Aussenac (CEO), Dick Dijkstra (co-CEO), Juan Jose Castiblanques (Chief Partnerships Officer) and Alex Pascal (Chief Strategy Officer). Each of you chooses your own coach, in French or English: each profile shows the languages that coach works in. Coaching starts in January 2027, after your leadership assessments.

Four focus areas
01

From founder to leader

Moving from building the company by doing to growing it by enabling others to do.

02

Leading through hyperscale

Naming the strain fast growth puts on a leadership team, and handling it early.

03

From founder-led to team-led

Clear roles, a shared vision, and the relationships that shift as the leadership team takes on more.

04

Coaching built on assessments

Each coaching plan starts from your leadership assessment, so the work builds on real data from day one.
